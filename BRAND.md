# ETHEREAL — Brand Design Sheet

## 1. Brand Voice
*Immaculate, architectural Parisian luxury meets hyper-precise neural generation.*

## 2. Color Palette
- **Obsidian Noir** (`#070707`): Primary deep background canvas.
- **Atelier Charcoal** (`#111113`): Surface containers, cards, and toolbars.
- **Elevated Monolith** (`#18181B`): Active states, hovering surfaces, and inputs.
- **Couture Alabaster** (`#FBFBFD`): High-contrast primary headings, primary buttons, and hero typography.
- **Titanium Mist** (`#8E8E93`): Muted secondary labels, studio notes, and metadata.
- **Cold Steel Hairline** (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`): Crisp structural dividers and borders.
- **Emerald Telemetry** (`#30D158`): Studio ready status, DuckDB BLOB indicator, and lightning render badges.

## 3. Typography
- **Display**: *Cormorant Garamond* (Weights: 300, 400, 500)
  *Role*: Atelier titles, Roman numeral navigation (`I · TRY-ON`, `IV · ROSTER`), and campaign hero mastheads.
- **Body & Controls**: *Outfit* (Weights: 300, 400, 500, 600)
  *Role*: Form inputs, toolbars, buttons, descriptions, and functional UI components.
- **Telemetry & Directives**: *JetBrains Mono* (Weights: 400, 500)
  *Role*: Prompt seeds, pixel aspect ratios (e.g. `768×1360`), latency timers, and DuckDB identifiers.

## 4. Signature Element
*The Optical Monitor*: A monolithic, reflection-capable studio viewport with architectural hairline borders, micro-dot telemetry, and instant DuckDB BLOB audit badges.

## 5. Motion Principles
- Transition curves: `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (couture ease-out).
- UI micro-interactions: 150ms–220ms.
- Full respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
